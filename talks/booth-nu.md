# Nu: the bank behind Monday's Pix infrastructure talk, and its claims on AI adoption

Booth visit: Nu · QCon SF 2026 · Wed Nov 18

> Nu (Nubank) is a Latin American digital bank, not a tool vendor; it sponsored QCon to hire engineers and gave a Monday talk on the infrastructure behind Brazil's Pix payments.

## Key points

- Nu was a Silver sponsor, and its sponsor listing links to Nubank's jobs board, so the booth was there to hire.
- Nu had a session on Monday: Cat Swetel, Senior Director of Engineering, on the runtimes, database topologies and zero-downtime designs behind Pix in the Architectures You've Always Wondered About track.
- Nu's newsroom says every employee has access to AI prototyping tools and that Nu measures their use and impact across functions.
- The same piece says AI-assisted development brought a backend architecture project in nearly a year early; it names no coding tools.
- Nu describes nuFormer, its own foundation model trained on customer financial data, as live in its largest Brazilian credit segment.
- Its new chief product officer said in July that AI had "completely transformed all of our workflows", with people keeping full oversight.

Booth, Wednesday Nov 18. Sources: QCon's sponsor page, the session page for Nu's talk and two Nu newsroom articles (June 11 and July 8, 2026), read Nov 18, 2026. Nu's figures are its own.

## What it is

Nu (Nubank) is a digital bank in Latin America, with Brazil as its largest market. It sells nothing to engineering teams. It was at QCon as an employer: its sponsor listing points to Nubank's jobs board, and it gave a talk on its payment infrastructure in the architecture track.

## What they say about AI

- **Everyone gets tools.** Every employee has AI prototyping tools, and use and impact are measured across functions.
- **Engineering.** A backend architecture project was accelerated by nearly a year with AI-assisted development. No tools are named.
- **Own models.** nuFormer, a self-supervised foundation model built on Nu's own data, makes credit decisions in its largest Brazilian segment and is expanding to Mexico and Colombia.
- **Research loop.** Its research team says automation cut the cycle for testing techniques from papers against internal data from months to one day.

## Where it connects to the talks

Nu's talk was [Architecting Nubank's Global Financial Infrastructure](https://qconsf.com/presentation/nov2026/architecting-nubanks-global-financial-infrastructure), Monday at 11:45 in Pacific DEKJ. It covered how Pix stays available under traffic spikes without over-provisioning, while meeting regulatory requirements.
